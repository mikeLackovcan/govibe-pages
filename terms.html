GoVibe Terms of Service & Safety Guidelines

By using GoVibe (app.govibe.mobile), you agree to abide by these terms and keep the solo-travel community safe and respectful.

1. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old to create an account or arrange travel meetups on GoVibe.

2. Safety-First Community Rules

  • Always meet new travel connections in public, well-lit venues and follow local laws.
  • Do not harass, spam, impersonate others, or share non-consensual photos or private personal information.
  • City-level check-ins are voluntary; use Ghost Mode whenever you prefer not to appear in a city list.

3. User Content

You retain ownership of photos, videos, and trip notes you post, and grant GoVibe a license to display them within the app according to your chosen visibility settings.

4. Simulated Trip Split / Ledger Tools

In-app bill split calculators and trip checklists are organizational convenience tools only; GoVibe does not process real-money payments, banking transfers, or escrow funds.
