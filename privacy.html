GoVibe Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how GoVibe (app.govibe.mobile), developed by Michal Fecko ("we", "our", or "us"), collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the GoVibe mobile and web applications.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Information: When you create an account or sign in, we collect your name/display name, email address, home/origin city, optional profile photo, and optional phone number if provided.
  • Travel Plans & City-Level Presence: Destinations, planned trip dates, visited country stamps, and manual city check-ins that you choose to post. GoVibe does not track or share precise live GPS coordinates.
  • User-Generated Content & Messages: Photos, short video clips, event drops, comments, and 1-on-1 or group chat messages you voluntarily upload or send inside the app.
  • Diagnostics & App Activity: Basic interaction state and error logs needed to keep app features reliable and secure.

2. How We Use Information

  • To authenticate your account and display your Traveler Passport profile.
  • To help solo travelers discover compatible travel dates, events, and safe city-level check-ins.
  • To deliver 1-on-1 direct messages, trip group chats, comments, and in-app activity alerts.
  • To maintain platform safety, prevent abuse, and respond to user support or deletion requests.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal information and we do not run third-party advertising SDKs. Infrastructure providers (such as Google Firebase / Supabase cloud storage and authentication services) process data only on our behalf under strict security and encryption-in-transit (HTTPS/TLS) standards.

4. Data Security & Retention

All network traffic between the GoVibe app and cloud servers is encrypted in transit using TLS/HTTPS. Local session data is stored securely on your device until you sign out or delete your account.

5. Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your GoVibe account and associated profile data at any time:

  • In the app: Open Passport → Account → Delete Account & Data.
  • Online / Email: Follow the instructions at GoVibe Account Deletion or email mike.fecko@gmail.com.

6. Age Requirement (18+)

GoVibe is intended exclusively for adult travelers aged 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about privacy or safety on GoVibe, contact Developer Michal Fecko at mike.fecko@gmail.com.
