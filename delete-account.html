Delete Your GoVibe Account & Data

This page describes how users of GoVibe (app.govibe.mobile) can request permanent deletion of their account and associated data.

Option 1: Delete Immediately Inside the GoVibe App

  1. Open the GoVibe app and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap the Passport tab (bottom navigation bar).
  3. Scroll to Account & Safety and tap Delete Account & Data.
  4. Confirm deletion to permanently remove your local session and cloud account record.

Option 2: Request Deletion via Email

Send an email from your registered address to mike.fecko@gmail.com with the subject "GoVibe Account Deletion Request" and include the email or display name linked to your account.

We verify ownership and complete full deletion within 7 business days.

What Data Is Deleted

  • User profile details (display name, email address, avatar URL, origin city).
  • Uploaded photos, short videos, city check-ins, and saved travel plans linked to your account.
  • Direct chat messages and notification records initiated by your account.
